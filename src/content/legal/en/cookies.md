## What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file saved by your browser when you visit a website. Some are essential for the site to work, others measure traffic or personalise advertising.

## Cookies we use

### Necessary (always on)

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `afx-consent` (local storage) | Remembers your cookie choices | 6 months |

### Analytics (with your consent)

| Name | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `_ga`, `_ga_<ID>` | Google Analytics | Anonymised visit statistics | 13 months maximum |

No analytics script is loaded until you accept it.

### Marketing (with your consent)

The website currently uses no marketing cookies. This category is reserved for possible advertising campaigns and will never be enabled without your consent.

## Managing your choices

You can change your preferences at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

To learn more about your data, read our [privacy policy](/en/privacy-policy/).
