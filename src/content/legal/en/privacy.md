## Data we collect

**Through the contact form**: full name, company (optional), email address, type of need and message.

- To answer your request and get back to you.

Processing is based on your consent, given by ticking the box on the form or by accepting cookies. You can withdraw it at any time.

## Your rights

Under law no. 09-08, you have the right to access, correct and object to the processing of your data. To exercise these rights, write to [contact@afriexport…] stating your request.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Moroccan data protection authority (CNDP): [www.cndp.ma](https://www.cndp.ma).

## Security

The website is served over HTTPS and exchanges with our providers are encrypted. Access to incoming requests is limited to the people who handle them.
